# Deck review

After writing or changing slides, read the whole deck once more as the audience would,
then record suggestions. Suggestions are for the person, not actions you take.

Raise a **content** suggestion when:

- a point from the brief is missing or thin because the knowledge base had nothing on it;
- a slide relies on `draft` knowledge that needs confirming;
- the brief and the knowledge base disagreed;
- the story has a gap: a claim without proof, or no clear ask at the end.

Raise a **design** suggestion when:

- a different palette, layout or image would serve a slide better than what was asked;
- two neighbouring slides look alike;
- a slide would benefit from an image the library does not have. Describe the image.

Keep each suggestion to one sentence that says what to do and why. Name the slide it
concerns. Raise at most five, most useful first, and none at all if the deck needs none.
Replace earlier suggestions that the latest changes have resolved.
