# Fossefall brand guide for presentations

Read by the deck agent before it designs anything. Derived from
`Fossefall_Brand_Guideline.pdf` (Commotion, 1 Nov 2025) and `Fossefall_template.pdf`.

## Who Fossefall is

Fossefall's identity channels the force of falling water: power, flow and renewal.
The vertical drop is its defining symbol. The logo's interlocking "S" stands for energy
converging into intelligence, a nod to waterfalls and data streams. The form language is
clean and Nordic: pure AI power shaped by clarity, sustainability and control.

Tagline: **pure AI power** (lower case, set in Space Mono under the logo).

## Voice

- Confident yet calm. State things plainly; no hype, no exclamation marks in body copy.
- Short sentences. One idea per slide.
- Concrete over abstract: a number, a place, a date beats an adjective.
- British English spelling ("colour", "centre").
- Never invent facts, figures, names or quotes. Every figure comes from the brief or the
  knowledge base. If the material is missing, write the slide without the figure and
  raise a suggestion asking for it.

## Typography

- **Inter** for all headlines and body text. Headlines are Regular weight, sentence case,
  tightly tracked, and usually end without a full stop. Single-word section openers may
  end with a full stop ("Introduction.").
- **Space Mono** for small details only: kickers, labels, captions, stat labels, page
  furniture. Never for headlines or paragraphs.
- Do not write headlines in all capitals. Kickers in Space Mono may be upper case.
- Headlines: aim for 3 to 8 words, never more than three lines.
- Body: at most about 45 words on a slide. If you need more, split the slide.

## Colour

The renderer applies colour from the deck's palette; you choose a palette for the deck
and a theme per slide, never raw hex values.

- Palettes: `fjord` (default; deep navy with sand-gold, as in the presentation template),
  `forest` (deep forest green with moss lime, the guideline's primary colours), and the
  seasonal sets `winter`, `spring`, `summer`, `autumn`.
- Use one palette per deck. Pick a seasonal palette only if the brief asks for it or the
  deck is tied to that season.
- Slide themes: `light` (soft grey, the default), `paper` (white), `tint` (warm off-white)
  and `dark` (the palette's dark colour with the accent for emphasis).
- Rhythm: mostly `light` and `paper`. Use `dark` for the cover, the closing slide and at
  most one or two moments of emphasis (a big number, a quote). Never two dark content
  slides in a row.
- The accent colour is for emphasis on dark slides and for small marks. Never set long
  text in the accent colour.

## Layout

Every slide carries the left rail (monogram at the top, vertical wordmark and copyright
at the bottom) and a page number. The renderer adds these; do not repeat the logo in
slide content.

- Generous white space. A slide should feel half empty.
- Asymmetric composition: text on one side, image or space on the other.
- Open with `cover`, follow with `agenda` for decks of seven slides or more, use `section`
  slides to open each part of a longer deck, and end with `closing`.
- Vary layouts. Never use the same layout three times in a row.
- Numbers get room: use `bigNumber` or `stats` instead of burying figures in paragraphs.

## Imagery

Norwegian nature, photographed plainly: forest, water, rock, mist, small human figures
seen from a distance for scale. No stock-style office photos, no AI-generated-looking
renders, no people facing the camera.

- Use only images listed in the brand's image library, by id.
- Match the image to the idea (see each image's `use` note). Do not use the same image
  twice in a deck unless the deck is longer than twelve slides.
- Portrait images go in `split` or `gallery` layouts. Only landscape images go full-bleed.

## Logo

- Wordmark and monogram appear only where the renderer places them: cover, closing and
  the rail. Do not describe or recreate the logo in text.
- Never place text over the logo, recolour it outside the palette, or stretch it.

## Open points to confirm with the brand owner

- The guideline's primary-colour page prints hex codes that do not match the swatches
  drawn beside them (for example "Moss #989B86" beside a bright lime). The `forest`
  palette uses the colours as drawn.
- The presentation template uses a navy and sand-gold pairing that is not named in the
  guideline. It is recorded here as `fjord`.
- The guideline shows an "S" repeat pattern. The renderer currently draws an
  approximation of it; replace with the supplied artwork when available.
