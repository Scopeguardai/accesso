Fossefall's tagline is "pure AI power".

Fossefall's identity channels the force of falling water: power, flow and renewal. The
vertical drop is its defining symbol, representing gravity and continuous motion.

The logo embodies flow, strength and precision. The interlocking "S" monogram symbolises
energy converging into intelligence, a nod to waterfalls and data streams. The wordmark's
geometric stability contrasts with the fluid emblem, expressing balance between nature
and engineered structure.

The clean, Nordic form language reflects Fossefall's identity: pure AI power shaped by
clarity, sustainability and control.
