Fossefall's stated direction is to move from infrastructure builder to full-stack compute
platform.

The company is building more than data centre sites: it is building the foundation of a
full-stack AI compute platform. Every SPV (special purpose vehicle) it develops
contributes to a consolidated, valuable infrastructure base.

Once critical mass is reached, the company expects to unlock platform-level scale:
monetising GPU capacity, capturing end-user margins, and securing tech-style valuations.
